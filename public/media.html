Dar Al Rimal opens Dubai headquarters at EMAAR Business Park

Dar Al Rimal International Petroleum Trading LLC has commenced operations from its new headquarters at Unit 5, Building 4, EMAAR Business Park on Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai — placing the company at the centre of the Gulf's energy trading corridor and within reach of the region's key ports, banks and counterparties.

15Aug
Trading

Refined products desk commences spot trading across MEA corridors

The company's refined products desk is now actively quoting gasoil, jet fuel and fuel oil across Middle East–Africa and Middle East–Asia trade routes. All shipments are executed on vetted tonnage with independent quantity and quality inspection at custody transfer.

Building a global counterparty network across three continents

Dar Al Rimal is establishing supply and offtake relationships with producers, refiners and distributors across Asia, Africa and Europe — anchored by a compliance-first onboarding process covering KYC, sanctions screening and financial due diligence.
