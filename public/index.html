Who We Are

A new force in international petroleum trading, built on timeless values

Headquartered at EMAAR Business Park on Dubai's Sheikh Zayed Road, Dar Al Rimal — "House of the Sands" — was founded to bridge the world's energy producers and consumers. We combine deep regional insight from the heart of the Gulf with a global outlook, disciplined risk management and an uncompromising standard of integrity.

  • Strategically located in Dubai, the commercial gateway between East and West
  • Physically focused — we trade real barrels, real cargoes, real relationships
  • Compliance first — rigorous KYC, sanctions screening and trade documentation

How We Work

From enquiry to delivery

A disciplined, transparent trading process — so counterparties always know where their cargo, documents and payments stand.

01

Source & Verify

We identify supply against demand, verify origin and specification, and complete full KYC and sanctions screening on every counterparty.

02

Structure & Contract

Commercial terms, incoterms, pricing and payment instruments are structured to protect both sides of the trade.

03

Ship & Inspect

Vetted vessels and independent inspectors (SGS-class) safeguard quantity and quality from load port to discharge.

04

Deliver & Settle

Cargoes are delivered on schedule, documents presented cleanly, and settlements executed through first-class banks.

Our Impact & Sustainability

Responsible trading for a changing energy world

Energy underpins human progress — and we believe it must be delivered responsibly. Dar Al Rimal is committed to the highest standards of health, safety and environmental care across our operations and supply chains, and to supporting the region's transition toward a lower-carbon future.

  • Zero-tolerance HSE culture across chartering, storage and logistics
  • Ethical sourcing and full traceability of every cargo we handle
