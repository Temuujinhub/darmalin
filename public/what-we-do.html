Diesel & Gasoil — EN 590

Diesel is the core of our refined products desk. We supply ultra-low-sulphur automotive diesel to the European EN 590 standard (10 ppm sulphur) and gasoil grades from 50 to 5,000 ppm to fuel distributors, mining and construction fleets, power producers, agriculture and transport operators. For cold-climate markets, including Mongolia and Central Asia, we offer summer, winter and arctic diesel with cold-flow properties (CFPP and cloud point) matched to the destination and season. Each cargo is certified by an independent inspector at load and discharge, and can be delivered by vessel, rail tank car or road tanker.

  • Diesel EN 590 · 10 ppm (ULSD)
  • Gasoil 50–5,000 ppm
  • Summer · Winter · Arctic grades
  • Marine gasoil (ISO 8217 DMA)
  • Vessel · Rail · Truck
