Physical trading, across the barrel

We source, trade and deliver petroleum products across three continents — pairing deep market knowledge with vetted logistics and bank-grade execution.

Products

Our trading portfolio

Every product line is run by specialists who understand its quality parameters, logistics constraints and price dynamics.

Crude Oil

We source and market crude oil grades from the Middle East and beyond, matching producer availability with refiner demand across Asia, Africa and Europe. Our desk covers light, medium and heavy grades on FOB, CFR and CIF bases, with flexible cargo sizes from parcels to full VLCC stems arranged through partners.

  • Light / Medium / Heavy grades
  • FOB · CFR · CIF
  • Spot & term structures
  • Independent inspection

Refined Products

Our refined products desk trades the middle and light ends of the barrel — gasoline, gasoil/diesel (including low-sulphur grades), jet fuel/kerosene and fuel oil — serving distributors, utilities, airlines' suppliers and bunkering hubs. Quality is certified at both load and discharge by internationally recognised inspectors.

  • Gasoline 92/95/98 RON
  • Gasoil 10–5000 ppm
  • Jet A-1
  • Fuel Oil 180/380 CST
  • VLSFO

Petrochemicals & Feedstocks

We supply petrochemical feedstocks and intermediates to crackers, aromatics complexes and polymer converters — including naphtha, condensates, aromatics (benzene, toluene, xylenes) and polymer grades. Scheduling reliability and specification discipline are the backbone of this desk.

  • Naphtha & condensate
  • BTX aromatics
  • PE / PP polymers
  • Methanol & MTBE

LPG & Gas Products

Propane, butane and mixed LPG for utilities, cylinder marketers and petrochemical users — shipped in pressurised and refrigerated parcels. We work with established terminal operators to ensure safe handling and dependable delivery windows.

  • Propane
  • Butane
  • Mixed LPG
  • Pressurised & refrigerated

Bitumen & Asphalt

Paving and industrial bitumen grades supporting road and infrastructure programmes across the Middle East, East Africa and South Asia — supplied in bulk, drums and bitutainers with penetration and viscosity grades to buyer specification.

  • Pen 60/70 · 80/100
  • Viscosity grades
  • Bulk · Drums · Bitutainers
  • Oxidised grades

Base Oils & Lubricants

Group I, II and III base oils sourced from leading refineries for lubricant blenders and distributors, alongside finished lubricants for automotive and industrial applications. Flexible parcels by flexitank, ISO tank and bulk vessel.

  • Group I / II / III
  • SN 150 · SN 500
  • Flexitank · ISO tank · Bulk
  • Finished lubricants
Services

More than a trading desk

Around every cargo we wrap the services that make physical trade work — so our partners can focus on their core business.

Shipping & Logistics

Chartering of vetted tonnage, voyage management and multimodal delivery — sea, road and rail.

Storage & Blending

Tankage arrangements at strategic hubs including Fujairah, with blending to buyer specification.

Risk Management

Price risk managed through disciplined hedging, exposure limits and independent oversight.

Trade Finance

LC-backed structures, documentary collections and open-account terms through first-class banks.
