GME Oman crude marker settles at $88.61 a barrel on 17 November

The Gulf Mercantile Exchange's official Oman crude futures marker price was $88.61 a barrel on 17 November 2026, up $0.31 from five sessions earlier.

Prices18 Nov 2026Source: Gulf Mercantile Exchange (GME)

The official Daily Marker Price for Oman crude futures (OQD) on the Gulf Mercantile Exchange (GME) was $88.61 a barrel on 17 November 2026, according to the Dubai-based exchange, formerly known as the Dubai Mercantile Exchange.

Over five trading sessions the marker was little changed. It rose $0.31 from its 10 November level, when it had fallen to $88.30/bbl. During the week the price recovered steadily over four consecutive sessions, then dropped back on 17 November and gave up most of those gains.

Across the 30 trading sessions since 7 October, the marker traded in a wide range. It peaked at $116.77 a barrel on 23 October, and its low for the period was the 10 November settlement. The price was still above $100/bbl at the start of November but declined sharply over the following days, so the latest level sits near the bottom of the period's range.

GME publishes the Daily Marker Price as the official daily reference for its Oman crude futures contract, quoted in US dollars per barrel.
