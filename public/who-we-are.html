The House of the Sands, with the world in view

Dar Al Rimal is a Dubai-headquartered international petroleum trading company — founded on the belief that global energy trade should be fast, fair and built on trust.

Our Story

Born in Dubai, built for global markets

Our name — Dar Al Rimal, the House of the Sands — honours the desert heritage of the Arabian Peninsula, where trade routes have connected civilisations for centuries. Today, we carry that legacy into modern energy markets.

From our headquarters at EMAAR Business Park on Sheikh Zayed Road, we operate at the crossroads of the world's energy flows: within reach of the Gulf's producing giants, the refining hubs of Asia, and the growing demand centres of Africa. Our team combines decades of collective experience in physical commodity trading, shipping, trade finance and risk management — brought together under one disciplined, entrepreneurial roof.

We are deliberately focused. Rather than chasing every market, we build deep, repeatable trade flows where we can add genuine value: reliable supply for buyers, dependable offtake for producers, and clean execution for everyone in between.

Our Mission

To connect the world's energy producers and consumers through transparent, dependable and efficient petroleum trading — creating value for our partners, our people and the communities we serve.

Our Vision

To be recognised among the Middle East's most trusted independent petroleum trading houses — the counterparty of choice for quality, integrity and performance across three continents.

Our Values

What we stand for

Four principles govern every trade we make and every relationship we build.

Integrity

We honour our word. Contracts, specifications and settlement terms are met precisely — even when markets move against us.

Reliability

Cargoes arrive on time and on spec. Our partners plan their businesses around our performance, and we protect that trust.

Agility

Markets shift in hours, not quarters. Our lean structure lets us price, decide and execute faster than larger houses.

Stewardship

We trade responsibly — protecting people, the environment and the reputation of the markets we operate in.

Governance & Compliance

Discipline behind every deal

Trading is a business of trust, and trust is earned through governance. Dar Al Rimal operates a compliance-first framework across every transaction.

  • Know Your Counterparty — full KYC/KYCC due diligence before any commitment
  • Sanctions & AML screening — every counterparty, vessel and cargo, every time
  • Documentary rigour — clean, bank-ready documentation on all shipments
  • Risk management — defined exposure limits, hedging discipline and independent oversight
