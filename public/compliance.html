Compliance is our licence to trade

International petroleum trading runs on trust, and trust runs on governance. Every counterparty, cargo and vessel we touch passes through a disciplined, auditable compliance framework.

Our Framework

Six controls on every transaction

01 /

KYC & KYCC Due Diligence

Full Know-Your-Counterparty and Know-Your-Counterparty's-Counterparty checks — corporate registry, UBO identification and financial standing — before any commitment is made.

02 /

Sanctions Screening

Every counterparty, vessel, port and cargo screened against UN, OFAC, EU and UK sanctions lists at onboarding and again before each fixture and payment.

03 /

AML & Trade Finance Integrity

Anti-money-laundering controls aligned with UAE regulation; settlements executed exclusively through first-class banks with transparent payment chains.

04 /

Documentary Standards

Clean, bank-ready documentation on every shipment — bills of lading, certificates of origin and quality, and inspection reports retained for full auditability.

05 /

Vessel Vetting

Chartered tonnage screened for age, class, P&I cover, inspection history and AIS integrity — dark-fleet tonnage is never fixed, at any price.

06 /

Risk Governance

Defined exposure limits per counterparty and market, disciplined hedging, and management oversight separated from the trading desk.

Code of Conduct

Zero tolerance, clearly stated

  • No bribery or corruption — in any form, in any market, under any commercial pressure
  • No sanctioned trade — cargoes of sanctioned origin or destination are declined outright
  • No opaque structures — we deal with identified principals, never undisclosed intermediaries
  • Full traceability — every barrel we handle can be traced from origin to discharge

UAE Licensed

Incorporated and licensed in Dubai as an international petroleum trading LLC.

Independent Inspection

SGS-class inspectors certify quantity and quality at every custody transfer.

Records & Audit

Transaction records retained and available to banking and regulatory partners.
