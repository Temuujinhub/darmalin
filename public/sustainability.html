Trading responsibly, today and tomorrow

Energy powers human progress. Our responsibility is to move it safely, ethically and with care for the people and places our business touches.

Our Pillars

Four commitments that guide every cargo

Health & Safety

Zero-harm expectations for every vessel, terminal and truck that carries our cargo. Safety is a condition of doing business with us.

Environment

Vetted tonnage, spill-prevention standards and support for lower-carbon fuels — including VLSFO and transition products.

Ethics & Governance

Full traceability, anti-corruption controls, sanctions compliance and transparent dealings with every counterparty.

People & Community

Investing in our team's development and supporting education and community initiatives in the UAE and our trading regions.

HSE In Practice

Safety is engineered into every trade

Because we work through chartered vessels, third-party terminals and logistics partners, our leverage is selection and standards — and we use it fully.

  • Vessel vetting — tonnage screened against international safety and inspection standards before fixture
  • Terminal & storage standards — partners assessed for containment, firefighting and emergency readiness
  • Cargo integrity — independent inspection of quantity and quality at custody transfer
  • Incident readiness — clear escalation and response procedures for every shipment
Energy Transition

Meeting today's demand, preparing for tomorrow's

The world will need oil and gas for decades — traded ever more cleanly and efficiently. At the same time, the energy mix is changing, and we intend to change with it.

  • Cleaner barrels — prioritising low-sulphur fuels and efficient logistics that cut emissions per tonne delivered
  • Transition-ready — building capability in gas, biofuel blending components and future fuels
  • Aligned with the UAE — supporting the nation's Net Zero 2050 strategic initiative through responsible practice
